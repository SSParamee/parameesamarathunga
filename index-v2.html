A user guide. A walkthrough video. An in-app tooltip. A course module. I build all of these. Not across different careers — in the same one.

The question I keep coming back to is simple: why do people finish content and still not know what to do next? Recognising something is not the same as understanding it. Understanding it is not the same as applying it. The gap between knowing and doing is where I chose to work.

Technical writing gives people an accurate place to start. Content strategy makes sure they find that place, in the right format, at the right moment. Content writing earns their attention long enough to matter. Learning design closes the distance between knowing and doing.

Seven years. Four disciplines. Different companies. I don't measure content by how well it reads. I measure it by what people can do when it's read and closed.
