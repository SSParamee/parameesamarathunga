SEO · GEO · AEO Content Framework
10+ websites · 2023–Present
Framework

Every web engagement operates across three layers simultaneously: SEO (ranking in traditional search), GEO (appearing in AI-generated answers on Perplexity, ChatGPT, and similar), and AEO (answering specific questions directly in featured snippets). Not add-ons — embedded into architecture from the first brief.

  • Page copy written with entity relationships and semantic clarity that AI models can parse and cite
  • FAQ and how-to structures embedded naturally — not bolted on
  • CTA architecture tied to conversion goals, not traffic metrics
  • SEO audits, keyword research, and competitor analysis preceding all content work

A distinct writing discipline most generalist copywriters cannot execute.

Technical sector expertise
